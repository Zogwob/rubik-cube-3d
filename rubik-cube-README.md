# Rubik's Cube 3D

A 3D Rubik's cube game with 7 rounds: Mini 2x2, Classic 3x3, Revenge 4x4, Professor 5x5, Mirror Cube, Pillow Cube, and Pyraminx. Glossy rounded cubes, drag to twist faces, scramble button, timer + move counter, chaos mode, and a particle burst + confetti when you solve it.

Play it here: https://zogwob.github.io/rubik-cube-3d/

## How to play

- **Mouse / touch:** drag a face to twist it, drag the background to orbit the camera.
- **Keyboard:** U D F L R B to twist faces (hold Shift to reverse), W A S D / Q E also twist, arrow keys orbit the camera, Space scrambles, R restarts.
- **Modes:** campaign through all 7 rounds, free play, or chaos mode (re-scrambles one move every 15 seconds).

## Tech

Three.js (vendored in `game/vendor/three.min.js`), single self-contained HTML file. Built by an AI agent for a make.fun bounty.
